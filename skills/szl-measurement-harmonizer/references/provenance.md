# Provenance and rights

The version 2 data-contract additions (provenance, declared-instant staleness, explicit
missing tokens, the `data_contract` report block, the `v2-*` fixtures, and
`tests/test_measurement_contract_v2.py`) are original SZL contributions to
`szl-holdings/szl-skills` under its Apache-2.0 license, written against base commit
`b8e259c`. No third-party code, dataset rows, or instrument output was copied; fixture
values and provenance strings are invented. Provenance fields are caller declarations,
not verified custody or rights. Scientific usefulness and host behaviour are `NOT_MEASURED`.
